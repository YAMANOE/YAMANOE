## About

I build AI systems for the public sector in Jordan — the kind that have to survive a real audit, not just a demo.

My work sits where **LLM agents meet hard, verifiable data**: geospatial road networks, Arabic NLP, and computer vision. I care most about systems that **refuse to answer when the evidence isn't there**, instead of inventing a number.

- 🎓 B.Sc. Data Science & Artificial Intelligence — Yarmouk University
- 🏛️ AI Developer, **9xAI Program** @ Al-Hussein Technical University (HTU)
- 🌐 I ship bilingual, RTL-first interfaces
- 🧊 3D visualization with Blender

---

## What I work on

**🚦 Traffic Intelligence Platform** — Amman
> A hierarchical multi-agent system that answers real planning questions: *"what happens if University Street closes?"* Built on a routable OpenStreetMap graph in PostGIS + pgRouting (135k car edges, 99k vertices), with a corridor registry, Arabic street-name resolution, and a critic layer that rejects any claim without evidence behind it.
>
> `TypeScript` `NestJS` `PostgreSQL/PostGIS` `pgRouting` `Ollama`

**🗣️ VOC-360 (صوت المواطن)** — multi-ministry citizen feedback
> A complaint intake and analysis platform serving Jordanian ministries. Arabic NLP pipeline, clustering, causal analysis, cross-ministry role-based access, and Arabic PDF reporting.
>
> `React` `FastAPI` `PostgreSQL` `Arabic NLP` `Docker`

**🛸 Aerial Traffic Analysis**
> Drone-based traffic data collection — detection, multi-object tracking, zone occupancy, origin–destination flows, and calibrated speeds from raw footage.
>
> `Python` `Supervision` `YOLO` `OpenCV`

**🌱 AgriBot AI** — graduation project
> Crop prediction and fertilizer recommendation from IoT sensor data. XGBoost at 99% accuracy, Random Forest at 94%, running on Raspberry Pi.
